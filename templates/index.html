What does OmniCare AI do?

OmniCare AI is an intelligent clinical assistant created to help emergency room doctors, nurses, and hospital managers make fast, safe, and accurate decisions during high-stress situations.

Even without medical training, understanding the core system is simple:

  • 1
    Emergency Patient Prioritization (Triage): The AI instantly reads patient vital signs and complaints to rank patients from most severe (life-threatening) to least severe.
  • 2
    Pharmacy Stock & Resource Tracking: The system continuously monitors hospital medication supplies, warning staff when key medicines are running low.
  • 3
    Visual Tissue & Healing Diagnostics: Using computer vision, it scans images of patient wounds or tissue to detect signs of infection and track recovery timelines.
